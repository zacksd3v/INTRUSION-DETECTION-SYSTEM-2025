FAQ

Is NIDS real-time?

Yes. Traffic is analyzed in real time and threats are detected and blocked instantly.

Can I self-host NIDS?

Yes. NIDS supports both cloud deployment and on-premise infrastructure.

What types of attacks does NIDS detect?

NIDS detects DDoS, brute-force, port scanning, malware traffic, and zero-day anomalies.

Does NIDS use machine learning?

Yes. It combines signature-based detection with AI-driven behavioral analysis.

How fast is the response time?

Average response time is under 50 milliseconds, even under high traffic load.

Can NIDS integrate with existing firewalls?

Yes. It integrates seamlessly with firewalls, SIEM tools, and SOC platforms.

Does NIDS generate alerts?

Yes. Alerts can be sent via dashboard, email, Slack, or webhook integrations.

Is NIDS suitable for enterprises?

Absolutely. NIDS is designed to scale from small teams to enterprise-level networks.

Can I customize detection rules?

Yes. You can create, modify, and tune detection rules to match your environment.

Is my data secure?

Yes. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest, following industry best practices.
